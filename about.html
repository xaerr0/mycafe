Our approach

Fresh food, made when you order it

Every plate at My Cafe is cooked to order with quality ingredients: organic coffee and tea, fresh squeezed orange juice, and scratch-made sides. We're a customer-driven business, and we're always working on getting a little better: the menu, the service, the room.

We deliver to Newark, Fremont, and Union City, and we're open seven days a week for breakfast and lunch.
