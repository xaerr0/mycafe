Newark, California

Family-run since 2010

A neighborhood diner built around fresh ingredients, made-to-order cooking, and treating every guest like a regular.

Every plate at My Cafe is cooked to order with quality ingredients: organic coffee and tea, fresh squeezed orange juice, and scratch-made sides. We're a customer-driven business, and we're always working on getting a little better: the menu, the service, the room.

We deliver to Newark, Fremont, and Union City, and we're open seven days a week for breakfast and lunch: Monday–Friday 8am–2:30pm, Saturday–Sunday 8am–4pm.

My Cafe has built its reputation one plate at a time since opening in 2010, earning a spot in Newark's top ten restaurants on Tripadvisor.
